# Custodian Accountability (Custodian Tab)

The Custodian tab manages faculty and staff profiles and tracks who is legally accountable for institutional property.

## Custodian Directory

Lists all registered faculty and staff with their account role (Full-time Faculty, Part-time Faculty, or Administrator), email address, and number of assigned assets.

## Property Acknowledgment Receipts (PAR / ICS)

Administrators can view and generate official Property Acknowledgment Receipts (PAR) and Inventory Custodian Slips (ICS) listing all equipment assigned to a custodian, formatted for printing and signature.

## Onboarding and Access Control

Administrators can invite new faculty members, switch accounts between active and inactive, and adjust system permissions.
