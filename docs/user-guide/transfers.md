# Asset Transfers & Chain of Custody (Transfer Tab)

The Transfer tab manages asset relocations between rooms and the reassignment of accountability between faculty members.

## Submitting a Transfer Request

Specify the asset, the destination room, the new accountable custodian, and the reason for the relocation.

## Approval and Reassignment

Department administrators review pending requests:

* **Approval**: The asset's room and custodian are updated automatically. The timestamp and approving administrator are recorded.
* **Rejection**: Administrative notes explaining the decision are required.

## Chain of Custody Log

Every asset keeps an immutable transfer history showing previous locations, previous custodians, new assignments, authorization timestamps, and administrative notes, supporting audit compliance.
