# Incident & Maintenance Tracking (Report Tab)

The Report tab manages equipment condition issues, repair tracking, and decommissioning using a 3-tab lifecycle.

## Lifecycle Workflow

1. **New**: Unread incident reports from faculty or custodians describing damaged, malfunctioning, or missing property.
2. **Pending (Under Action)**: Tickets acknowledged by administrators and being resolved. Status badges show the action taken:
    * **For Repair**: Assigned to an internal or external technician.
    * **For Replacement**: A procurement request has been started for a replacement unit or component.
    * **For Condemnation**: The asset is scheduled for official write-off and disposal.
3. **Closed**: Finished tickets with a resolution badge:
    * **Resolved (Working)**: Repaired or replaced and returned to active room inventory.
    * **Condemned (Retired)**: Written off and permanently decommissioned.

## Filing an Incident Report

Faculty members and custodians can file a report by selecting the asset, describing the issue, indicating its current condition, and optionally attaching photo evidence.
